Privacy Policy | Cashtrix

Effective date: 3 October 2026

Cashtrix ("we", "us", "our") is a personal finance tracker. This policy explains what data the Cashtrix mobile app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The same document is linked from inside the app (Login, Register, Profile) and from the store listing.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address and password hash, managed by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth).
  • Financial data you enter: wallets, transactions (amount, date, note), categories, budgets, display name, currency and timezone preferences.
  • Avatar image: only if you upload one; stored in private per-user storage.
  • Receipt photos: only if you photograph a receipt in the Add form; stored in private per-user storage and automatically deleted 30 days after capture. If you ask the app to read a receipt, the photo is sent to our server for automated reading only after your explicit consent; the result only pre-fills the form, and nothing is saved until you press Save yourself.
  • AI prefill (Phase 1): if you use voice entry or receipt scan, the app sends only dictated text (max 500 characters) to the parse-voice function, or only the stored photo reference (storage_path, never bytes) to the scan-receipt function. The server adds your active wallets, visible categories, and timezone as context, then asks Gemini to return a suggestion (amount, kind, wallet and category hints, note; or amount, merchant, category hint for receipts). Processing is temporary: nothing is saved until you press Save yourself, no temporary files are created, and server logs record codes only, never text or amounts. Speech itself stays with your OS (keyboard microphone or typed text); no audio is recorded or uploaded by the app except the voice recording you explicitly choose to make (next item). The confidence value shown is a fixed honest marker (0.42), not a measured accuracy score. Speech dictated through the OS keyboard or typed by hand stays with your OS. (Audio you choose to record is covered separately below.)
  • AI voice recording (Phase 2): only if you tap record in voice entry, after a separate explicit microphone consent. The app records at most 15 seconds of audio and uploads it to a private per-user drafts area. The server transcribes it with the transcribe-voice function, validates the result like any other suggestion, then deletes the file immediately: zero retention, no table, the transcript itself is never stored. Server logs record codes only, never audio or amounts. The result only pre-fills the form, and nothing is saved until you press Save yourself.
  • Two-factor authentication (optional): if you turn it on, a one-time-code factor is registered with Supabase Auth; recovery is the email password reset.
  • App lock (optional): a device-only on/off flag. Unlocking uses your device biometrics or device passcode; no biometric data ever leaves your device and the app stores no secret.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and anonymous usage events (screen views, transaction saved, budget threshold reached). These events never contain transaction amounts or notes.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the app: sign-in, balances, history, budgets, analytics.
  • To read the receipts you choose to scan, only to pre-fill the form.
  • To turn your dictated text into a form suggestion, only to pre-fill the form.
  • To transcribe a voice recording you choose to make, only to pre-fill the form.
  • To keep your account secure and to send account-related email (verification, password reset).
  • To fix crashes and improve stability using aggregated diagnostics.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising.

3. Storage and security

  • App data is hosted on Supabase (Postgres) with row-level security: every query is scoped to the signed-in user; unauthenticated access is denied.
  • Passwords are stored as hashes by Supabase Auth; we never see them.
  • Your session token lives only on your device and is wiped on sign-out.
  • Avatar files live in a private bucket, readable only by their owner. Receipt photos live in a separate private bucket with the same owner-only access, and are deleted automatically 30 days after capture. Voice drafts live in a separate private area with the same owner-only access, and are deleted by the server immediately after transcription (zero retention).

4. Your rights: export and deletion

Inside the app (Profile) you can export all your transactions as CSV, and you can delete your account. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, wallets, transactions, budgets, receipt photos, avatar, and login | this cannot be undone.

5. Children

Cashtrix is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; accounts found to belong to under-13 users will be deleted.

6. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be announced in the app or on the repository before they take effect. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
